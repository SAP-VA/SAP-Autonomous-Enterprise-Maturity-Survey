Data powers the Autonomous Enterprise

Trusted data turns AI from an experiment into enterprise execution.

In an Autonomous Enterprise, AI agents understand business context, coordinate work across functions, execute approved processes, and continuously optimize outcomes. That requires connected data, shared meaning, clear ownership, and governance by design.

With those foundations in place, organizations can move faster without giving up control: people retain strategy, judgment, and oversight while AI takes on more coordination and execution.

Ground agents in trusted data

Keep enterprise data accurate, relevant, current, consistent, and accountable so agents can act on reliable information.

Connect context to execution

Give agents secure access to shared definitions, relationships, and processes across SAP and third-party systems.

Scale autonomy with control

Embed permissions, policies, lineage, quality controls, and traceability into every agent interaction and action.

The value of the Autonomous Enterprise

AI coordinates and executes at scale. People focus on strategy, judgment, and innovation.

When agents are grounded in enterprise context and governed from the start, business signals can trigger approved action across systems—helping the organization respond faster while maintaining compliance, transparency, and accountability.

What the assessment evaluates

Six data capabilities behind Autonomous Enterprise readiness

Rate the capability your organization can repeat across the enterprise—not the best isolated pilot. Each answer reveals how the data foundation supports or limits agentic AI at scale.

Data ownership

Priority data is tied to high-value agentic use cases, with accountable business owners and reusable data products.

Data governance

Policies, permissions, lineage, agent access, write-back controls, and evidence are embedded by design.

Data quality

Enterprise data is accurate, relevant, complete, current, and consistently represented across systems.

Business context

Shared definitions, relationships, metadata, lineage, and unstructured knowledge give agents meaning.

Data accessibility

Approved data is discoverable, reusable, and available at the speed required by AI decisions.

Data architecture

A modular, interoperable foundation connects analytics, applications, and AI through stable interfaces.

Agent capability requirements

What agents need to operate across the enterprise

These four capabilities enable agents to understand context, coordinate work, execute approved actions, and improve outcomes without bypassing enterprise governance.

Business context

Agents need shared definitions, master data, relationships, policies, and process history to understand what each business signal means.

Coordinate across boundaries

Pass context between agents, people, functions, and SAP or third-party systems without losing meaning or control.

Execute approved work

Complete routine process steps, update systems, and route exceptions according to defined permissions and policies.

Improve from live outcomes

Monitor results, identify changing conditions, and adapt execution within human-defined performance and risk limits.

Human oversight remains central

People remain responsible for strategy, ethical judgment, exception handling, accountability, and organizational leadership.

Autonomous Enterprise value

What the future state can unlock

The value appears when trusted data is connected directly to governed business execution.

Return capacity to higher-value work

Agents can gather information, reconcile routine differences, update records, and coordinate handoffs so employees spend more time on decisions, customers, and innovation.

Respond at the speed of the business

A supply disruption, payment exception, or demand shift can trigger approved cross-functional action immediately instead of waiting for reports and meetings.

Scale execution without losing control

Every agent uses scoped access, follows policy, records its evidence, and routes defined exceptions to people—making faster execution traceable and auditable.

Before you begin

Data powers the Autonomous Enterprise

A house is only as strong as its foundation. Two-thirds of enterprises worldwide have experimented with agentic AI, but without proper data capabilities in place, fewer than 10 percent have scaled towards tangible value.1

How data enables agentic AI

Agents gain clear context

Reliable, current data replaces fragmented and conflicting inputs with the context agents need to drive sound decision-making.

Agents turn insights to action

Discoverable, connected data lets agents work across processes, respond in real time, and move from analysis to execution.

Agents scale with confidence

Clear ownership and embedded governance keep every action secure, traceable, and grounded in a trusted source of truth.
